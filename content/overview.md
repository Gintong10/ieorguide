# Infernal Eclipse of Ragnarok: Class Progression Guide

Updated 7 October 2026
## How to use this guide

Pick your class page and read down: each one lists weapons, armor and accessories for all 22 progression stages, from the first night to the endgame superbosses. This overview page holds what every class shares: the boss order, how the seven classes work, and where each list comes from. Gear that works for any class is on the Shared gear page.

- A stage is named for the boss it prepares you for. "Pre-Skeletron" is the gear you can reach before fighting Skeletron.
- Loadouts follow the modpack's own wiki, which assumes Ragnarok Mode, wherever it has a list, and the pack's installed mod files plus the Calamity wiki where it does not. They are menus of viable picks, not one best build.
- The modpack lets you skip ahead and fight bosses out of order. The order here is the intended one.
- Every item says where it comes from. On a class page, hover or tap an item to see its recipe, the enemy or boss that drops it, or who sells it; switch on "Show where to get each item" to list them all at once.

| Marker | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| † | Risky or hard to get at this stage; prepare before going for it |
| C | Crowd control: strong in events and against worm bosses |
| + | Support or secondary pick: debuffs enemies or helps the team |
| ≤ | Has variants or upgrades that are also worth using |
| \* | Tedious to obtain at this stage |
| ν | Needs the Void subclass from Secrets of the Shadows |
| Ω | Meant to be used together; a number ties specific items to each other |
| Δ | Works differently in Calamity than in vanilla, such as a new set bonus |
| ≈ | Placed by rarity alone from the mod files; can be a stage off |
| *Italic* | Only obtainable on Revengeance Mode or higher |

## The modpack at a glance

Infernal Eclipse of Ragnarok is a compatibility mod that turns Calamity and its add-ons, Thorium, Secrets of the Shadows and Consolaria into one Infernum-difficulty campaign. Its wiki lists 0.10.8 as the current version.

- **Ragnarok Mode** is the pack's own difficulty: Master Mode plus Infernum, with further boss changes on top.
- **One boss ladder.** Each mod's bosses are slotted into a single order, shown in the next section. Thorium's Primordials, for example, move from just after Moon Lord to after the Devourer of Gods.
- **Re-tiered items.** The pack rebalances items and merges crafting trees across mods, so an item can sit earlier or later here than on its home wiki.
- **Companion mods.** Infernal Arsenal adds weapons, Thrower Unification folds Thorium's thrower into Rogue, and Calamity/Thorium Balancing evens out the two mods.

* **Versions used.** As of 7 October 2026: tModLoader 2026.08 with 55 mods enabled, including Infernal Eclipse 0.10.8, Calamity 2.2.2, Thorium 1.7.2.8, Secrets of the Shadows 0.25.1.9, Catalyst 1.1.8, Consolaria 2.2.3, Ragnarok 1.6.0.8 and Calamity Bard & Healer 0.13.10.

## The seven classes

Four classes are vanilla, Rogue comes from Calamity, and Healer and Bard come from Thorium. Each has its own page.

| Class | From | Runs on | What sets it apart | Loadouts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Melee | Vanilla | No resource | Close and short range, behind the heaviest armor | Melee |
| Ranged | Vanilla | Ammunition | Bows, guns and launchers; ammo choice changes per fight | Ranged |
| Magic | Vanilla | Mana | Staves, tomes and magic guns; needs mana regeneration gear | Magic |
| Summoner | Vanilla | Minion and sentry slots | Minions fight for you. Calamity cuts summon damage to 0.75x if you also use another class's weapon | Summoner |
| Rogue | Calamity, merged with Thorium's thrower | Stealth, plus technique points and exhaustion | Thrown weapons with a stealth strike; builds split into spam and stealth | Rogue |
| Healer | Thorium | Mana | Scythes and wands for damage, plus tools that heal you and allies | Healer |
| Bard | Thorium | Inspiration | Instruments that grant timed empowerments | Bard |

Every class also has a Void subclass from Secrets of the Shadows, which spends a separate void resource. Items marked ν need it.

## Where each list comes from

Each list uses the most specific source that exists for it: the modpack's own wiki where it has written the class, the pack's installed mod files for everything it has not, and the Calamity wiki's curated picks for the four Calamity classes.

| Class | Stages 1 to 6 | Stages 7 to 15 | Stages 16 to 22 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Melee | Pack wiki | Pack wiki | Pack wiki |
| Ranged | Pack wiki | Pack wiki at stage 7, then Calamity wiki picks plus other mods from the mod files | Calamity wiki picks plus other mods from the mod files |
| Magic | Pack wiki | Calamity wiki picks plus other mods from the mod files | Calamity wiki picks plus other mods from the mod files |
| Summoner | Pack wiki | Pack wiki at stage 7, then Calamity wiki picks plus other mods from the mod files | Calamity wiki picks plus other mods from the mod files |
| Rogue | Pack wiki | Calamity wiki picks plus other mods from the mod files | Calamity wiki picks plus other mods from the mod files |
| Healer | Pack wiki | Mod files | Mod files |
| Bard | Pack wiki | Mod files | Mod files |
| Shared gear and all-class armor | Pack wiki | Pack wiki, Melee list | Pack wiki, Melee list |

- **How the mod-file lists were built.** The 22 content mods of a tModLoader install were decompiled on 7 October 2026, covering about 3,900 weapons, armor pieces and accessories. Each is placed by its recipe ingredients or the boss that drops it, after applying the 126 recipe changes and 675 item changes in the pack's Calamity/Thorium Balancing mod.
- **Computed, not playtested.** Expect most placements to be right and some to be a stage off. ≈ marks the ones placed by rarity alone.
- **Complete, not ranked.** Mod-file lists show everything a class can get at a stage, not what is best. Damage numbers help compare similar weapons but ignore attack speed and projectile behaviour.
- **Calamity picks match the pack's version.** The pack loads Calamity 2.2.2 while the wiki tracks 2.2.4; the two versions' item lists match name for name.
- **Where items come from.** Recipes, boss and enemy drops, treasure bags and shop stock are read from the same mod files and from the game's own code, with the pack's recipe changes applied. Where an item has several recipes the first is shown. Chest loot, fishing catches and a few vanilla boss drops are filled in by hand. Drop chances and shop conditions are mostly left out, so check the in-game Bestiary or the recipe browser for those.
- **Tooltips win.** The pack rebalances items; where an in-game tooltip disagrees with a list here, trust the tooltip.

Accessories, potions and permanent upgrades that suit any class are in Shared gear.
